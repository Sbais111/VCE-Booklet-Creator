    Before you paste

    • Use Claude with file creation on. It needs to build and send back Word files.
    • One Area of Study per chat. Start a new conversation for each booklet.
    • Paste key knowledge. Copying the dot points from the study design keeps questions inside the course.
    • Turn on web search. It lets Claude look up past VCAA exams and examination reports.
    • Drafts stay in this browser. Each subject keeps its own draft on this device only.
    Unit titles, Areas of Study and outcome statements follow the VCAA VCE Physical Education and VCE Health and Human Development study designs (accredited 2025–2029). Always check against the current study design on the VCAA website.
